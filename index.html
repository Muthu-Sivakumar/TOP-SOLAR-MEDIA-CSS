Welcome!

What is Solar Media?

I made Solar Media to share about a vision that gets me excited - Solarpunk. The website's name itself is a play on "Social Media". As such, I am designing this website to grow a platform for Solarpunk enthusiasts.

That's cool and all, but what's Solarpunk?

Glad you asked!

Solarpunk is both an aesthetic and hope for the future. It doesn't have a formal definition, but many sources define it as a future built on sustainable technology, harmony with nature, community, and decentralized infrastructure.

I want to emphasize that Solarpunk isn't about a specific technology or urban plan - it is about building towards a future you want to live in.

For me, that future is community-driven, integrated with nature, and self-sustaining. For you it may be different. To each their own!

Important: Aesthetics vs Implementation

With any vision, it is easy to get caught in its aesthetics:

  • Easy to motivate yourself on Gymtok, hard going to the gym
  • Easy to daydream about your dream lifestyle w/o putting effort towards building it
  • Easy to speak a plan outloud, hard to follow through with it

I don't want this website to be the same.

This website is focused on the "how" of Solarpunk. What roads do we face in making Solarpunk a reality? What innovations show promise? Why haven't we approached this vision yet?

That said, aesthetics has a place in Solarpunk's conversation. Aesthetics turns Solarpunk from an imaginary concept into a tangible vision.

In fact, aesthetics was what drew me into Solarpunk in the first place. I'd even argue Solarpunk is only worth building towards because of its aestheic - who'd want to work towards something that doesn't fill them with wonder!

These ideas in mind, this website will focus on the implementation of Solarpunk over its aesthetic, but will do its best to convey its beauty for those new and to continue to inspire people towards building it out!
