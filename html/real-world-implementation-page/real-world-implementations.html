Real-world Implementations

What are "Real-World Implementations?"

"Real-world Implementations" is about how ideas in Solarpunk are being built in this world. From large-scale solar panel installations, established community-driven urban designs, and blockchain technologies, anything Solarpunk-related that has been tangibly built into the world falls under this page.
