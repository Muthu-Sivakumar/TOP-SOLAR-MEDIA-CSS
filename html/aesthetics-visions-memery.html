Aesthetics, Visions, and Memery

What are "Aesthetics and Visions"?

"Aesthetics and Visions" refer to any expression of hope associated with Solarpunk. "Aesthetics" is about a tangible image, like pixelart, hand drawings, and digital media. "Visions" refer to articulated visions people have for a Solarpunk future. This can be through poems, articles, essays, whatever conveys the vision someone has for this kind of world. I hope this serves as a space for creatives to inspire others with their work.
