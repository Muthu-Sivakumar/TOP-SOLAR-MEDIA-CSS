Solarpunk

What is "Solarpunk"

"Solarpunk" is a vision for a more hopeful future. It doesn't have a univesral definition, but it is commonly associated with sustainable technology, living alongside nature, community-driven design, and decentralized infrastructure. As emphasized in the "Home" page, this vision isn't tied down to any specific technology or idea, it is about your vision for a better future. This looks different for everyone, and to each their own

I want this page to serve as an introduction to Solarpunk for those interested. Overviews of common topics in Solarpunk, its history, and where it stands today are all things I'd like to cover on this page.

To a better future.
