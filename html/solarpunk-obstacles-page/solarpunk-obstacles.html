Solarpunk Obstacles

What are "Solarpunk Obstacles"?

Perhaps the most relevant page to what I hoped this website would be about, "Solarpunk Obstacles" is about understanding what is holding back a Solarpunk future. What are we missing, are there misaligned incentives, is there inadequate support for these projects? Questions like these are adressed in this page.

Being someone who truly understands these obstacles will move Solarpunk from an airy-fairy vision of our world to something that may actually become our future.

I wish we can be curious, rigorous, and unbiased in these conversations.

Seek truth! Gain knowledge!
